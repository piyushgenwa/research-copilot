# Mobile Checkout Usability

Study: Mobile Checkout Usability
Participants: P12, P17, P21

Participant P17:
"I usually abandon checkout when I have to re-enter my card details. I don't understand why the app doesn't remember my payment method."

Researcher observation:
Participants frequently expressed frustration with repeated payment entry.

Participant P12:
"I thought I had already paid because I entered my details on the previous screen."

Researcher observation:
The transition between entering payment details and confirming the order was unclear to some participants.
