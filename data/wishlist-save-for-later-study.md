# Wishlist and Save for Later

Study: Wishlist and Save for Later
Date: 2026-04-29
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P82, P83, P84, P85, P86, P87

Participant P82:
"I use the heart button as a to-do list. When payday comes I go through it and buy two or three things."

Participant P83:
"I hearted something and later couldn't find where the hearts go. Is it under my account? Under the basket?"

Participant P84:
"I'd love to share my wishlist with my partner before my birthday. Right now I screenshot things and send them."

Participant P85:
"Things disappear from my wishlist when they sell out. I'd rather it stayed with a 'sold out' tag so I know."

Researcher observation:
The wishlist is used as a planning tool tied to budget and paydays, but it is hard to find and items vanish silently.

Participant P86:
"Save for later in the basket is really handy. I move things there when the total is too high."

Participant P87:
"I never use wishlists. If I want it I buy it, if I don't I forget about it."

Researcher observation:
Wishlist users and non-users were clearly distinct groups. Users asked for sharing and back-in-stock alerts.
