# Older Adults Usability Study

Study: Usability with Adults Aged 65 and Over
Date: 2026-05-02
Method: In-person moderated usability test
Participants: P161, P162, P163, P164, P165, P166

Participant P161:
"The buttons are so small. I pressed the wrong one three times and ended up in a different page."

Participant P162:
"I don't like putting my card into phones. My grandson set it up and I let him do the buying."

Participant P163:
"Is there a phone number I can call? I'd feel much better talking to a real person."

Researcher observation:
Small touch targets caused frequent mis-taps. Several participants wanted a phone support option before trusting the app with payment.

Participant P164:
"I actually find it easier than the website. Bigger pictures, fewer things on the screen."

Participant P165:
"The little icons without words, I don't know what they mean. Put the word under it."

Participant P166:
"It logged me out and I don't remember my password. That's where I'd give up."

Researcher observation:
Icon-only controls and forced re-login were barriers. Unlike younger participants in other studies, this group preferred labelled icons over a minimal interface.
