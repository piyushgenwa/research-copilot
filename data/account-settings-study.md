# Account Settings Usability

Study: Account Settings Usability
Date: 2026-03-18
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P45, P46, P47

Participant P45:
"I wanted to change my email address and I couldn't find where. I checked profile, settings, security."

Participant P46:
"Deleting my account took forever. I had to email support, there was no button."

Participant P47:
"The password reset link took me back to the login page, which asked me to reset my password. I went round in a loop."

Researcher observation:
Account management tasks are hard to find and some flows dead-end. Settings information architecture needs review.
