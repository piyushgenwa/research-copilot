# Loyalty Program Research

Study: Loyalty Program Research
Date: 2026-03-25
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P54, P55, P56

Participant P54:
"I have 2,340 points. I have no idea what that's worth in money."

Participant P55:
"I only joined because it gave me ten percent off my first order. I haven't looked at it since."

Participant P56:
"My points expired and nobody told me. That made me pretty angry."

Researcher observation:
The value of points is unclear and expiry is not communicated. Most participants treat the programme as a one-off discount.
