# Product Reviews and Ratings

Study: Product Reviews and Ratings
Date: 2026-02-10
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P57, P58, P59

Participant P57:
"I scroll straight to the reviews with photos. Stock photos don't tell me anything."

Participant P58:
"I want to filter reviews to see just the one star ones. That's where you learn what actually goes wrong."

Participant P59:
"When every review is five stars and says 'great product!!' I assume they're fake."

Researcher observation:
Customer photos and access to critical reviews are key trust signals. Uniformly positive reviews reduce credibility.
