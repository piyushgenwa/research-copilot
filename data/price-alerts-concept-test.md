# Price Drop Alerts Concept Test

Study: Price Drop Alerts Concept Test
Date: 2026-06-09
Method: Moderated concept test
Participants: P132, P133, P134, P135, P136, P137

Participant P132:
"Yes! I'd set an alert on the TV I've been eyeing and wait for it to drop."

Participant P133:
"Only if it's an email, not another push notification. I get enough of those."

Participant P134:
"I'd want to set my own target price, not just 'any drop'. A one pound drop isn't worth telling me about."

Researcher observation:
Price alerts were one of the most welcomed concepts tested this year. Participants wanted control over the threshold and the channel.

Participant P135:
"If the price drops right after I buy, will you refund the difference? That's what I'd really want."

Participant P136:
"I'm suspicious. Shops raise the price and then 'drop' it. Show me the price history so I know it's real."

Participant P137:
"It would make me delay buying. Not sure that's good for the shop, but it's good for me."

Researcher observation:
Price history was requested to verify that discounts are genuine. Post-purchase price protection came up unprompted.
