# Payment Trust AI-Moderated Interviews

Study: Payment Trust AI-Moderated Interviews
Date: 2026-06-30
Method: AI-moderated interviews, transcript
Participants: P167, P168, P169, P170, P171, P172

Moderator:
Walk me through what you check before you tap Pay.

Participant P167:
"The total, and that the card ending is the right one. I've got a personal and a work card saved."

Participant P168:
"Honestly nothing. If it's a shop I know, I just tap."

Moderator:
What happens in your head right after you tap Pay?

Participant P169:
"I hold my breath until I see the green tick. If it takes more than a couple of seconds I start to worry I've been charged twice."

Participant P170:
"Once it just went back to the home screen with no confirmation. I checked my banking app to make sure it went through."

Researcher observation:
The moment after tapping Pay remains a source of anxiety. This is consistent with the earlier checkout and checkout redesign studies, where missing or slow confirmation caused hesitation.

Moderator:
Would you use a saved card here?

Participant P171:
"Yes, as long as I can see which card it is and remove it whenever I want."

Participant P172:
"No. I don't save cards anywhere after my details were stolen last year."

Researcher observation:
Attitudes to saved cards remain split, matching the payment methods study. Visible card details and easy removal made saving acceptable to some.
