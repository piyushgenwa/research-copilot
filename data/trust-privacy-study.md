# Trust and Privacy Research

Study: Trust and Privacy Research
Date: 2026-01-12
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P51, P52, P53

Participant P51:
"Why does a shopping app need my location all the time? That feels creepy."

Participant P52:
"I'd share more data if they told me what I get in return. Right now it's just a wall of legal text."

Participant P53:
"When I see a padlock and a known payment brand at checkout I feel safer entering my card."

Researcher observation:
Participants want plain-language explanations of why data is collected. Visible security cues at payment increase willingness to transact.
