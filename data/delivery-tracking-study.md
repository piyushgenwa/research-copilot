# Delivery Tracking Research

Study: Delivery Tracking Research
Date: 2026-02-17
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P33, P34, P35

Participant P33:
"I love the live map. Watching the driver get closer is weirdly satisfying."

Participant P34:
"The estimated delivery time said 2pm and it arrived at 6pm. I stayed home all afternoon for nothing."

Participant P35:
"I didn't even know my order had shipped until it showed up. The status still said processing."

Researcher observation:
Tracking status was sometimes out of date, which undermined trust in delivery estimates. Accurate, timely status updates matter more than the map.
