# Guest Checkout Interviews

Study: Guest Checkout and Forced Sign-Up Interviews
Date: 2026-05-12
Method: Moderated interviews, transcript
Participants: P77, P78, P79, P80, P81

Moderator:
Tell me about the last time you started buying something in the app and didn't finish.

Participant P77:
"It made me create an account before I could pay. I just wanted one thing, so I closed it and bought it on the website of another shop."

Moderator:
What would have made you stay?

Participant P77:
"A guest option. Let me pay first, then ask if I want an account."

Participant P78:
"I don't mind making an account, actually. I like having my order history in one place."

Moderator:
How did the sign-up step feel when you did it?

Participant P79:
"Too many fields. Date of birth? Phone number? For a phone case? I left halfway through."

Participant P80:
"Signing in with Apple was quick. If that's there I don't really care."

Researcher observation:
Forced account creation before payment caused drop-off for three of five participants. Participants were more willing to create an account after purchase, or when a one-tap social sign-in was available.

Participant P81:
"I get a hundred marketing emails from every account I make. That's why I avoid them."

Researcher observation:
Fear of marketing email is a hidden reason for avoiding sign-up.
