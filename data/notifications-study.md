# Push Notifications Study

Study: Push Notifications Study
Date: 2026-02-03
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P30, P31, P32

Participant P30:
"I get three or four promo notifications a day. It's too much, it feels like spam."

Participant P31:
"The shipping notifications are actually useful. I like knowing when my order has left the warehouse."

Participant P32:
"I turned off all notifications in settings after the first week because I couldn't pick which ones I wanted."

Researcher observation:
Participants value transactional notifications such as order and delivery updates but are annoyed by the volume of marketing notifications. Lack of granular controls led some to disable everything.
