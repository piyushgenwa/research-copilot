# Referral Program Research

Study: Referral Program Research
Date: 2026-05-15
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P127, P128, P129, P130, P131

Participant P127:
"I'd refer friends if I actually liked the shop. The money isn't why I'd do it."

Participant P128:
"The referral link was long and ugly. When I sent it on WhatsApp it looked like spam."

Participant P129:
"I referred two friends and never got my credit. There's no way to see if it worked."

Researcher observation:
Participants could not track the status of referrals, which led to distrust when rewards did not appear.

Participant P130:
"Give both of us something. If only I get a reward it feels like I'm selling my friends."

Participant P131:
"Referral codes are fine. I used one to get my first discount and I'd share mine the same way."

Researcher observation:
Two-sided rewards and a visible referral status were the main requests.
