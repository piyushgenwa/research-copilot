# Product Comparison Usability

Study: Product Comparison Usability
Date: 2026-05-20
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P88, P89, P90, P91, P92, P93

Participant P88:
"I opened three tabs of headphones and flicked between them. I wish I could just put them side by side."

Participant P89:
"Oh, there's a compare button? I never saw it. It's tiny and grey."

Participant P90:
"The comparison table is great but it shows forty rows. I only care about battery, noise cancelling and price."

Researcher observation:
The comparison feature exists but is rarely discovered. Once found, the table is too long to be useful.

Participant P91:
"I compare by reading reviews, not specs. The specs all sound the same to me."

Participant P92:
"Highlight the differences. If both have Bluetooth 5.3 I don't need to see that row."

Participant P93:
"I'd compare on my laptop, not on my phone. The phone screen is too small for a table."

Researcher observation:
Participants asked for a differences-only view and a shorter set of key attributes. Some prefer reviews over specifications for comparison.
