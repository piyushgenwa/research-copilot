# Personalisation Quiz in Onboarding

Study: Personalisation Quiz in Onboarding
Date: 2026-05-28
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P138, P139, P140, P141, P142, P143

Participant P138:
"Five questions about my style before I can see anything? Just show me the shop."

Participant P139:
"I liked the quiz, it was quick and the home page after actually looked like my taste."

Participant P140:
"I wasn't sure if I could skip it. There was no skip button that I could see."

Researcher observation:
The quiz split participants. Those who completed it saw better recommendations, but the lack of a visible skip option frustrated others and delayed their first look at products.

Participant P141:
"It asked my budget. That felt a bit personal for the first minute of using an app."

Participant P142:
"I answered randomly just to get through it, so the recommendations were rubbish."

Participant P143:
"Let me do the quiz later, from my profile, when I actually care."

Researcher observation:
Participants who rushed the quiz gave low-quality answers. Offering it later, after initial browsing, was suggested. This echoes the follow-up onboarding study finding that early permission requests get declined.
