# Mobile Onboarding Usability

Study: Mobile Onboarding Usability
Participants: P03, P08, P11

Participant P03:
"I wasn't sure what the next step was after I created my account. I kept looking for a button to continue."

Researcher observation:
Several participants hesitated after account creation because the primary next action was not visually obvious.

Participant P08:
"The screens look nice, but I didn't know whether I was finished or supposed to keep going."

Researcher observation:
Participants wanted clearer progress and completion cues.
