# Onboarding Follow-up Study

Study: Onboarding Follow-up after Progress Indicator Launch
Date: 2026-04-08
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P48, P49, P50

Participant P48:
"The progress bar at the top helped. I could see I was on step 3 of 4 so I knew I wasn't done."

Participant P49:
"I skipped the tutorial straight away. I just want to use the app."

Researcher observation:
The new progress indicator resolved most of the uncertainty about next steps observed in the earlier onboarding study. Participants no longer hesitated after account creation.

Participant P50:
"It asked for my location and notifications before I'd even seen what the app does. I said no to both."

Researcher observation:
Permission requests shown before users understand the app's value are being declined.
