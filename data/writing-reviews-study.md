# Writing Reviews Study

Study: Writing Product Reviews
Date: 2026-04-24
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P144, P145, P146, P147, P148

Participant P144:
"The review request email came two days after delivery. I hadn't even used the thing yet."

Participant P145:
"I tried to add photos to my review and it failed twice. I gave up and just left stars."

Participant P146:
"I only write reviews when something is really good or really bad. Never for average stuff."

Researcher observation:
Review requests arrive too early for products that need time to evaluate, and photo upload failures reduce the number of photo reviews, which other studies show buyers value most.

Participant P147:
"I left a two star review and it never appeared. Makes me think they hide bad reviews."

Participant P148:
"A small discount for reviewing would get me to do it. Otherwise I can't be bothered."

Researcher observation:
Moderation delays for negative reviews were interpreted as censorship.
