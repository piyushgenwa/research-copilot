# Payment Methods and Wallets

Study: Payment Methods and Wallets
Date: 2026-01-20
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P27, P28, P29

Participant P27:
"If there's Apple Pay I'll always use it. Typing a sixteen digit number on a phone is painful."

Participant P28:
"I don't like apps storing my card. I'd rather type it each time than worry about a data breach."

Researcher observation:
Attitudes to stored cards were split. Convenience-focused participants wanted saved cards and wallets; security-focused participants preferred manual entry.

Participant P29:
"The pay later option confused me. I couldn't tell if I was signing up for a loan or not, and what the fees were."

Researcher observation:
Buy-now-pay-later options need clearer explanation of fees and commitments.
