# Tablet and Cross-Device Use

Study: Tablet and Cross-Device Use
Date: 2026-04-22
Method: Contextual interviews in participants' homes
Participants: P66, P67, P68

Participant P66:
"I added things to my basket on my phone at lunch, then opened the app on the iPad at night and the basket was empty. I had to add everything again."

Participant P67:
"On the tablet it just looks like a stretched phone app. Loads of white space."

Participant P68:
"I browse on the tablet on the sofa but I always check out on my phone because that's where my payment details are saved."

Researcher observation:
Basket and saved details do not sync across devices, forcing participants to repeat work. The tablet layout is not optimised.
