# Pricing Survey Open-Ended Responses

Study: Subscription Pricing Survey
Date: 2026-04-01
Method: Online survey, open-ended responses
Participants: R101, R102, R103, R104, R105

Respondent R101:
"Too expensive compared to competitors for what you get."

Respondent R102:
"Annual plan is good value but I didn't realise it auto-renews."

Respondent R103:
"Would like a family plan."

Respondent R104:
"Hard to compare the tiers, the feature list is very long."

Respondent R105:
"Price is fair."

Researcher observation:
Open-ended responses echo the interview findings: difficulty comparing plans and uncertainty about renewal terms. Price level itself was mentioned less often than clarity.
