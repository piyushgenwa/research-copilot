# Accessibility Interviews

Study: Accessibility Interviews with Assistive Technology Users
Date: 2026-01-28
Method: Remote interviews with screen reader and magnification users
Participants: P39, P40, P41

Participant P39:
"With VoiceOver, half the buttons in checkout just say 'button'. I have no idea which one places the order."

Participant P40:
"I use large text on my phone and the app ignores it. Prices get cut off and some labels overlap."

Researcher observation:
Unlabelled controls and lack of dynamic type support block assistive technology users from completing core tasks.

Participant P41:
"The picture captcha at sign up is impossible for me. I had to ask my son to create the account."
