# Search Autocomplete Diary Study

Study: Search Autocomplete Diary Study
Date: 2026-05-25
Method: One-week diary study
Participants: P112, P113, P114, P115

Participant P112 (Day 1):
"Typed 'bluet' and it suggested bluetooth speakers straight away. Nice."

Participant P112 (Day 5):
"It keeps suggesting things I searched for once, weeks ago. I searched for a baby gift one time and now it's all baby stuff."

Participant P113 (Day 2):
"Suggestions show pictures now, which helps. I tapped the product directly instead of going to results."

Participant P114 (Day 3):
"Spelled 'moisturiser' the British way and it still found everything. Good."

Participant P114 (Day 6):
"I wanted to delete my recent searches and couldn't find how. Bit embarrassing when my friend used my phone."

Participant P115 (Day 4):
"The suggestions jump around while I'm typing and I tap the wrong one."

Researcher observation:
Autocomplete improved speed and handled spelling variants, but personalised suggestions based on one-off searches were unwelcome and search history could not be cleared easily. Shifting suggestion lists caused mis-taps.
