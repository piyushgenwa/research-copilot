# Gifting and Gift Cards

Study: Gifting and Gift Cards
Date: 2026-03-14
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P122, P123, P124, P125, P126

Participant P122:
"I sent a gift to my mum and the receipt with the price went in the box. Awkward."

Participant P123:
"Gift wrap option was hidden at the very end. I'd already decided not to bother by then."

Participant P124:
"Digital gift cards are perfect for my nephew. He picks what he wants, I don't have to guess."

Researcher observation:
Participants worried about prices being revealed to recipients. Gift options appear too late in checkout to influence decisions.

Participant P125:
"I couldn't use my gift card and my credit card together. So I had to buy something cheaper to use it up."

Participant P126:
"Scheduling the gift email for her birthday morning was lovely. She woke up to it."

Researcher observation:
Split payment with gift cards is a gap. Scheduled delivery of digital gifts was praised.
