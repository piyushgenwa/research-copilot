# Subscription Pricing Research

Study: Subscription Pricing Research
Participants: P04, P09, P15

Participant P04:
"I understand the monthly price, but I'm not sure what happens if I cancel."

Participant P09:
"The price is easy to find, but I couldn't tell what features are included in the plan."

Researcher observation:
Participants understood the headline price but wanted clearer information about cancellation and plan contents.

Participant P15:
"I'd like to compare the plans side by side before choosing."
