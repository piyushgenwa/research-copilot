# Checkout Redesign Evaluation

Study: Checkout Redesign Evaluation
Date: 2026-03-12
Method: Moderated usability test
Participants: P23, P24, P25, P26

Participant P23:
"Oh nice, it already has my card saved. That was so much quicker than last time."

Participant P24:
"The card was saved but it still made me type my delivery address again. Why does it remember one and not the other?"

Researcher observation:
Saved payment methods removed most of the frustration seen in the earlier checkout study. Participants completed payment faster and nobody abandoned at the card step.

Participant P25:
"I pressed Pay and then I wasn't sure if it went through. The spinner just kept going and there was no confirmation screen for a while."

Participant P26:
"Checkout feels fine now. I'd use it again."

Researcher observation:
The order confirmation step remains unclear for some participants. Two of four hesitated after tapping Pay because there was no immediate feedback.
