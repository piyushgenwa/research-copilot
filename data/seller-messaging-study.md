# Marketplace Seller Messaging

Study: Marketplace Seller Messaging
Date: 2026-06-16
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P149, P150, P151, P152, P153

Participant P149:
"I asked the seller if the lamp came with a bulb. No reply for four days, so I bought elsewhere."

Participant P150:
"I didn't know if I was talking to the shop or to a seller. Who is responsible if it goes wrong?"

Participant P151:
"The seller replied in minutes and even sent a video of the item. That's why I bought it."

Researcher observation:
Seller response time directly affected purchase decisions. Participants were unclear about who is responsible for marketplace orders.

Participant P152:
"I'd like to see 'usually replies within an hour' before I bother asking."

Participant P153:
"The chat notifications got mixed up with order notifications. I missed the seller's answer."

Researcher observation:
Showing typical seller response times and separating message notifications from order updates were requested.
