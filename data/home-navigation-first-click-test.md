# Home Screen Navigation First-Click Test

Study: Home Screen Navigation Redesign First-Click Test
Date: 2026-06-02
Method: Unmoderated first-click test with follow-up questions
Participants: P105, P106, P107, P108, P109, P110, P111

Researcher observation:
Task "Find your past orders": 5 of 7 participants clicked the profile icon first; 2 clicked the menu. Success within two clicks was 71%.

Participant P105:
"Orders feel like they belong to me, so I went to my profile."

Participant P106:
"I expected orders in the menu with everything else. The profile is for settings."

Researcher observation:
Task "Find today's deals": only 3 of 7 participants found the deals section first try. Most scrolled past the carousel because it looked like an advert.

Participant P107:
"That big banner? I thought it was an ad so I ignored it."

Participant P108:
"The bottom bar is clear. Home, search, basket, profile. I don't need anything else."

Participant P109:
"There's too much on the home screen. Deals, stories, recommendations, categories. I don't know where to look."

Participant P110:
"I liked the recommendations, they were actually things I'd buy."

Participant P111:
"I always just use search. I never browse the home page."

Researcher observation:
The bottom navigation bar performed well. The home feed was seen as cluttered, and promotional banners suffered from banner blindness.
