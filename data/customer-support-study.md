# Customer Support Experience

Study: Customer Support Experience
Date: 2026-02-24
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P42, P43, P44

Participant P42:
"The chatbot kept sending me help articles. I just wanted to talk to a person."

Participant P43:
"I gave the bot my order number, then when I finally got a human they asked for it again. I had to explain everything twice."

Researcher observation:
Having to repeat information when escalating from the bot to a human agent was a major source of frustration.

Participant P44:
"The agent replied within a couple of minutes and sorted out my refund. Honestly better than I expected."
