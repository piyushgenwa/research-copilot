# Subscription Cancellation Study

Study: Subscription Cancellation Study
Date: 2026-04-15
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P60, P61, P62

Participant P60:
"Cancelling took five screens of 'are you sure?' and offers. It felt like they were trying to trap me."

Participant P61:
"I would have paused instead of cancelling if there had been a pause option. I'm only away for two months."

Participant P62:
"I got charged for another year without any reminder. I only noticed on my bank statement."

Researcher observation:
Cancellation friction and unannounced renewals damage trust. Participants asked for pause options and renewal reminders. This connects to the cancellation uncertainty seen in the earlier pricing research.
