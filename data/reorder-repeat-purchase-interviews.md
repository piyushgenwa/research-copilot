# Reorder and Repeat Purchase Interviews

Study: Reorder and Repeat Purchase Interviews
Date: 2026-04-18
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P116, P117, P118, P119, P120, P121

Participant P116:
"I buy the same nappies, the same washing liquid, the same dog food. A 'buy again' button saves me every week."

Participant P117:
"The buy again list is sorted weirdly. The thing I bought once in 2024 is at the top."

Participant P118:
"I set up a subscription for coffee but I kept running out before the next delivery. I couldn't change the frequency easily."

Researcher observation:
Repeat purchase features are heavily used for household staples. Participants wanted the list ordered by how often they buy, and more control over subscription timing.

Participant P119:
"When the price goes up on something I reorder, I want to know. It crept up and I didn't notice for months."

Participant P120:
"Reordering is the one thing this app does better than the supermarket app."

Participant P121:
"I reordered and it sent the old size that's discontinued. It should have told me."

Researcher observation:
Silent changes to reordered items (price increases, discontinued variants) damaged trust.
