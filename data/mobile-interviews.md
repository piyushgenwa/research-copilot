# Mobile App Interviews

Study: General Mobile App Interviews
Participants: P02, P06, P19

Participant P02:
"I like being able to do things quickly from my phone, but I get annoyed when I have to repeat information I've already given."

Participant P06:
"Sometimes I don't know if something saved. I wish the app would just tell me."

Researcher observation:
Participants valued speed and clear feedback about system state.
