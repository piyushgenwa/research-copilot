# Click and Collect Store Pickup

Study: Click and Collect Store Pickup
Date: 2026-04-10
Method: Contextual interviews at pickup points
Participants: P100, P101, P102, P103, P104

Participant P100:
"The app said ready for pickup, but when I got there they couldn't find it for twenty minutes."

Participant P101:
"Pickup is great because I don't have to be home. I grab it on the way back from work."

Participant P102:
"I didn't know which door to use or where the counter was. A little map or photo would help."

Researcher observation:
The 'ready for pickup' status was sometimes sent before the order was actually ready, eroding trust in the notification.

Participant P103:
"I had to show the order number and my ID. Just give me a QR code to scan."

Participant P104:
"I liked that I could see the opening hours and pick a time slot. Very smooth."

Researcher observation:
Participants valued the convenience of pickup but were let down by inaccurate status and unclear in-store wayfinding. A scannable code was requested.
