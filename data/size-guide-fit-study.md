# Size Guide and Fit Study

Study: Apparel Size Guide and Fit
Date: 2026-03-30
Method: Semi-structured interviews
Participants: P94, P95, P96, P97, P98, P99

Participant P94:
"I always order two sizes and send one back. It's annoying but it's the only way to be sure."

Participant P95:
"The size guide is in centimetres and I have no idea what my chest measurement is. Who measures themselves?"

Participant P96:
"What helps me is 'fits small' or 'fits large' from other buyers. That's more useful than a chart."

Researcher observation:
Participants deliberately over-order to manage fit uncertainty, which drives return volume. Peer fit feedback was trusted more than size charts.

Participant P97:
"The model is 6 foot and wearing a medium. I'm 5 foot 4. That doesn't help me."

Participant P98:
"If you remembered my size from the last thing I kept, that would save me so much time."

Participant P99:
"The size guide was actually good on the jeans. It had waist and leg length, and they fit perfectly."

Researcher observation:
Size guides worked for structured items like jeans but not for tops and dresses. Remembering a customer's previous size was a strong request.
